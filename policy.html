Sudoku Flux – Brain Puzzle requests only permissions necessary for its current functionality and integrated services.

Internet Access

android.permission.INTERNET

Internet access is used for online functionality such as Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, Firebase database services, advertisements, and other network-dependent functionality.

Network State

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

This permission allows the App to determine whether an internet connection is available before performing network-dependent operations.

Vibration

android.permission.VIBRATE

The App may use vibration or haptic feedback during gameplay and interface interactions, such as number entry, game events, or other feedback.

This permission does not provide access to personal information.
