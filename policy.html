This Privacy Policy describes how Sudoku Master ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

Sudoku Master is a Sudoku puzzle game that provides standard Sudoku puzzles and other game modes and features.

By using Sudoku Master, you acknowledge the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The information processed by Sudoku Master depends on the features you choose to use. Some information is provided directly by you, while other technical information may be processed automatically by services used by the App.

1.1 Account and Authentication Information

Sudoku Master includes Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In functionality.

If you choose to sign in or create an account, information associated with authentication may be processed, including, depending on the sign-in method:

  • Email address or other account contact information
  • Firebase user identifier
  • Google account identifier when Google Sign-In is used
  • Basic account/profile information made available by the selected sign-in provider
  • IP address
  • Authentication tokens
  • Device and platform information required for authentication

We use this information to authenticate users, maintain accounts, associate game information with an account where applicable, protect authentication systems from abuse, and provide account-related features.

We do not receive or store your Google account password.

1.2 Game and App Data

Depending on the features you use, Sudoku Master may process game-related information such as:

  • Game progress
  • Puzzle state
  • Game scores
  • Statistics
  • Game mode information
  • Achievements or similar progress information
  • Settings and preferences
  • Other information necessary to provide game functionality

Some of this information may be stored locally on your device. If online or account-based functionality is used, applicable information may also be stored using Firebase services.

1.3 Technical and Device Information

The App and its third-party services may automatically process certain technical information, including:

  • IP address
  • Device and account identifiers where applicable
  • Advertising identifiers where available and permitted
  • Device model and device metadata
  • Operating system and platform information
  • App version and App identifiers
  • User-agent information
  • App and advertisement interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used to provide App functionality, authenticate users, display and measure advertisements, detect fraud and abuse, maintain security, and improve the reliability of services used by the App.

2. Android Permissions

Based on the current version of Sudoku Master, the App requests the following Android permissions:

Internet

android.permission.INTERNET

Internet access is required for online functionality such as Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, Firebase database services, advertising, and other network-dependent features.

Network State

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

This permission allows the App and its services to determine whether network connectivity is available before performing internet-dependent operations.

Vibration

android.permission.VIBRATE

The App may use vibration or haptic feedback for gameplay interactions, such as number entry, mistakes, game events, or other user-interface feedback.

The vibration permission does not provide access to personal information.

No Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Location, or Media Permission: Based on the current Android manifest, Sudoku Master does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise/approximate location, or photos/videos/storage access permissions.

3. Firebase Authentication

Sudoku Master uses Google Firebase Authentication for account and sign-in functionality.

Firebase Authentication may process information such as IP addresses, Firebase user identifiers, authentication information, user-agent information, App identifiers, and contact/account information associated with the authentication method selected by the user.

This information is used to provide authentication, account management, security, and abuse-prevention functionality.

4. Google Sign-In

Sudoku Master includes Google Sign-In.

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide information necessary to authenticate your account and provide the sign-in functionality. The exact information available depends on the permissions and scopes requested by the App.

Google processes information associated with your Google account according to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Firebase Cloud Firestore

The App includes Firebase Cloud Firestore.

Firestore may be used to store and retrieve App or game-related information required by features of Sudoku Master.

When a user is authenticated, Firebase may associate database requests with the applicable Firebase user identifier.

The exact game or account information stored in Firestore depends on the features of the App that use the database.

6. Firebase Realtime Database

Sudoku Master also includes Firebase Realtime Database.

Firebase Realtime Database may process technical information such as IP addresses and user-agent information. When Firebase Authentication is used, authenticated database requests may also contain the applicable Firebase user identifier.

The service may also process game or App information that Sudoku Master intentionally stores in the database to provide its functionality.

7. Advertising

Sudoku Master uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share certain information for purposes including advertising, analytics, ad measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

This may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • App and advertisement interactions
  • Information about App launches, taps, and similar interactions
  • Diagnostic information
  • Performance information
  • Device and account identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers where available and permitted

Advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable law, user consent, device settings, Google settings, and the configuration of advertising services.
